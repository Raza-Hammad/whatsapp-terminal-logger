# Turabees — Brand & Service Profile

Source: official website pages www.turabees.com (home, collections, bespoke, contact), reviewed 2026-10-20.

## Identity
- Turabees is a wedding couture atelier specialising in bespoke bridal and groomswear.
- Positioning: "Wedding Couture, Shaped Around Your Celebration" — timeless tradition balanced with modern style.
- Offers distinctive suits, sherwanis, prince coats, waistcoats and bespoke occasionwear, personalised in silhouette and finer details.
- Official website: https://www.turabees.com
- Spelling: Turabees (capital T). Common misspellings include Turabis, Turabes, Turabeez, Turabiz, Turabies, Turabess, Turbis.

## Collections
- Waistcoats — modern-cut waistcoats for men, shaped around wedding plans.
- Signature Sherwanis — grand couture sherwanis balancing traditional details with personal style.
- Prince Suits / Prince Coats — structured silhouettes, distinctive collars and occasionwear with individual character.
- Signature Suits — bespoke suits shaped around wedding plans.
- Also referenced: Tuxedo / Evening Wedding Tuxedo, Dinner Suit, Two-Piece Suit, Three-Piece Suit, Double-Breasted Suit.
- Bespoke builder categories on the site: Suits, Sherwanis, Prince Suits, Tuxedo.

## Fabrics & Materials
- The collections use European wools, Italian silks, artisanal handwoven brocades, authentic mill fabrics and heritage weaves.
- Fabric selection guidance covers colour, texture, fabric weight, appearance, movement and comfort.
- The Turabees fabric workbook (`fabrics.xlsx`) contains the working fabric dataset:
  - `fabrics.csv` — 3,603 data rows (full fabric catalogue).
  - `suit fabrics` — 3,612 data rows (suit-focused catalogue view).
  - `Final 90 fabrics` — 112 data rows (curated final selection).
  - `Final Sehrwani fabrics` — 218 data rows (sherwani / waistcoat / prince-coat fabrics).
  - `accessories` — 2 data rows (Royal Embroidered Stole, Heritage Zari Khussa).
  - `Image generation pipeline` — 49 data rows.
  - `Sheet6` — 1 data row.
  - Across the four fabric sheets there are 7,545 data rows and 3,831 unique catalog codes.

## Bespoke Process
1. Share your vision — occasion, preferred styles and wedding date.
2. Choose your details — fabrics, colours and design options.
3. Confirm your measurements — follow measurement guidance and review design, price and timeline.
4. Plan your delivery — confirm destination and delivery arrangements with the team.
- The website's bespoke studio also offers category/cut/fabric selection, embroidery level (None, Light, Medium, Heavy), optional design notes and an "AI Match with Bride" preview feature.
- Consultations are private 90-minute fitting/consultation appointments.
